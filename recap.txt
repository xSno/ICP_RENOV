ICP RENOV - CONTRACT MANAGEMENT APP - S8 RECAP

Implementation
- Migration 8 preserves migrations 1-7 and adds contracts.predecessor_contract_id, the terminal-status projection, bounded integrity checks and unique event constraints for renewal periods, non-renewal periods, termination, expiry and abandonment. No new ContractEvent type or event table was introduced.
- Every post-signature lifecycle decision uses SIGNATURE_RECORDED.document_id and the immutable snapshot of that exact signed revision. A newer unsigned Rxx and live master changes have no authority; missing or inconsistent signed data fails closed.
- The current period is the signed initial period extended only by contiguous RENEWAL_CONFIRMED events. The current price is the signed initial price replaced only by the latest confirmed renewal price; initial signed pricing and every document snapshot remain immutable.
- Internal attention and contractual notice deadline are separate date projections. Neither creates an event or sends a communication.
- MANUAL exposes “Préparer le renouvellement” and creates an independent editable DRAFT with predecessor_contract_id, no number and only CREATED. Proposed dates are contiguous, conditions/equipment come from signed authority rather than live masters, and an archived/incompatible historical model is not reused. Its own number is allocated later by the normal generation pipeline.
- A linked successor remains visibly identified while it is a DRAFT by the persistent in-workspace notice “Brouillon prérempli depuis le contrat précédent. Vérifiez les dates, les équipements, le régime et les conditions.” The notice survives step changes, is absent from ordinary/predecessor/non-DRAFT contracts, remains informational and creates no ContractEvent.
- TACIT never renews automatically. “Confirmer la reconduction” explicitly appends one RENEWAL_CONFIRMED with exact contiguous period, HT, controlled VAT, calculated VAT and TTC, without creating an Rxx/document. FIXED reuses the current-period price; NEW_PRICE_ON_RENEWAL records the confirmed HT/VAT. Multiple periods are supported generically.
- “Enregistrer une fin de contrat” appends RENEWAL_NOTICE_RECORDED for the exact current tacit period, records the notification date/note, sends nothing and causes expiry only at that period end. An unresolved TACIT contract neither auto-renews nor auto-expires.
- NONE and MANUAL expire at their current contractual end when still unresolved. TACIT expires only after an explicit non-renewal notice. EXPIRED is date-driven and idempotent.
- SIGNED/ACTIVE contracts support a bounded scheduled termination with exact effective date, reason, optional notification date and note. Future termination preserves the current status until due; effective termination appends TERMINATED once. A termination due before activation prevents activation, and termination takes priority over expiry.
- DRAFT/TO_SIGN use “Abandonner le contrat”, never résiliation. ABANDONED preserves number, documents and history. TERMINATED, EXPIRED and ABANDONED are terminal and structurally locked in the normal workflow.
- Reconciliation runs at the existing startup/refresh/open entry points, processes activation before a later termination when both are overdue, respects pre-activation termination priority, and is repeat-safe.

Remaining S8 risks only
- Date-driven lifecycle reconciliation is bounded to local application entry points; when the application is not opened, a due transition is persisted at the next successful entry point rather than by a background scheduler.
- Corrupt or incomplete signed snapshots/event histories intentionally fail closed and require technical repair; the UI never substitutes live masters or a newer unsigned revision.
- Notice timeliness is recorded but not legally evaluated, and S8 intentionally sends no message and generates no renewal/termination document.
- Visual states are prepared for manual Windows inspection; screenshot automation is intentionally absent.
