ICP RENOV - CONTRACT MANAGEMENT APP - S2 RECAP

Migration / schema
- Migrations 1 and 2 are unchanged. Explicit migration 3 adds contracts and contract_equipment_items.
- Migration 2 -> 3 and repeated initialization are deterministic and idempotent; latest schema version is 3.
- contracts supports DRAFT / CLIMATE_MAINTENANCE, nullable Contract-only regime, source references, typed JSON snapshots, signatory and timestamps. No business number or revision exists.
- contract_equipment_items persists stable ids, source references, unique contiguous positions, deterministic snapshots and Contract-only observations.

Contract domain / repository / service
- domain/contract.py defines the controlled enums, typed client/site/equipment snapshots, Contract aggregate, ordered item and list projection.
- repositories/contracts.py owns SQL, the snapshot-based list query and atomic client/site cascades, reorder, observation and signatory writes.
- services/contracts.py owns empty draft creation/reopen, active master selection checks, snapshot orchestration, master creation reuse, cascades, equipment selection/order and Contract-only edits.
- The application composition root injects ContractService; UI widgets contain no SQL or database construction.

DRAFT and snapshots
- Nouveau contrat persists an incomplete DRAFT immediately with an opaque UUID only; empty drafts survive restart and are never auto-deleted.
- Client, site and equipment snapshots serialize deterministically and exclude master internal notes/references not intended as contract-facing data.
- Existing drafts and the Contracts list read stored snapshots, not live master rows. Later master edits or archives do not silently rewrite or remove selected Contract data.

Cascades and signatory
- Changing client atomically replaces its snapshot/reference, resets the signatory prefill, clears regime, site, equipment and observations; master rows remain untouched.
- Changing site atomically replaces its snapshot/reference and clears equipment plus observations; master rows remain untouched.
- PERSON prefill is « prénom nom » / « Client ». ORGANIZATION uses the proposed contact when present and otherwise remains blank.
- Contract signatory edits never update the master client, and later master-contact edits never update an existing draft.

Ordered equipment and observations
- Only active equipment from the selected site can be newly selected; cross-site and archived selections are rejected by the service.
- Selections append last. Deselection removes its observation and compacts positions. Monter/Descendre persists contiguous order across restart.
- Equipment created from the Contract reuses MasterDataService, is automatically selected and appended last.
- Contract observations are stored only on contract_equipment_items; equipment.internal_notes is neither copied nor mutated.
- Already selected archived equipment remains visible from its Contract snapshot.

Contracts UI / workspace
- The Contrats placeholder is replaced by a bounded persisted draft landing with Nouveau contrat, snapshot-derived client/site labels, Brouillon, last update and reopen.
- The Contract header shows Retour aux contrats, Brouillon, Brouillon sans numéro, snapshot context and the real save state.
- Exactly four steps exist: Client, site & équipements; Conditions du contrat; Revue; Documents & suivi. Only step 1 is enabled/functional.
- Step 1 provides separate select/change and create actions for Client/Site, Contract signatory fields, equipment checkboxes/order/observation and the zero-equipment anomaly.
- Selectors and reused S1 master editors are embedded right-side drawers with French actions and Escape/cancel closure; no QDialog/top-level editor is introduced.
- No regime, conclusion mode, model, Conditions content, generation, numbering, revision or Delete action is exposed.
