- Root cause B: company.registered_address had no prepared mapping. It now uses the explicit canonical value, or the deterministic rendered structured company address; no business value is invented.
- Root cause C: breach_cure_period_days was omitted from the contract snapshot. The persisted secondary Conditions value is now copied and an active blank/invalid duration fails required-value preflight.
- Root causes D/E: refrigerant_partner_name and withdrawal_contact were absent from the reviewed smoke company snapshot. Active PARTNER/withdrawal controls now fail before rendering when their required values are empty.
